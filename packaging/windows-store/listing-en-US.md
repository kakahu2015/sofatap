## Product name

HostTap Remote

## Short description

Turn your iPhone into a wireless mouse, keyboard and text remote for your Windows PC.

## Description

HostTap Remote makes your iPhone the mouse and keyboard for your PC, so you can drive a living-room computer from the sofa without a separate keyboard, mouse or trackpad.

Install the receiver on the PC, open the HostTap app on the iPhone, and scan the QR code shown in the receiver window. Everything runs over your own local network — no account, no sign-in, no cloud relay.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Move the pointer, click, double-click, drag and scroll from the phone surface.
- Type with the phone keyboard, including supported dictation, then review the text and send it once.
- Reconnect to the saved PC later without scanning again.
- Keep the PC's own display in view. The receiver never mirrors or streams the screen.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install HostTap Remote and open it. The window shows a QR code and the local address it is listening on.
2. On the iPhone, choose Scan QR Code and point it at the receiver.
3. Confirm the PC name, then start controlling.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

- No screen streaming, screen recording or remote desktop.
- No unattended automation; every action comes from a live phone session.
- No lock-screen unlock and no Windows Hello bypass.

REQUIREMENTS

- Windows 10 version 1809 or later, x64 or arm64.
- The HostTap iPhone app on a phone connected to the same local network.
- Both devices on the same reachable home or office network.

PRIVACY

Pairing credentials stay on the device. No account data is collected and no telemetry is sent to a server. See the privacy policy for details.
