Privacy policy for HarmonyOS

How the HostTap HarmonyOS app handles information when you use a HarmonyOS phone as a touchpad and keyboard for your own computer.

Effective October 21, 2026

WindowsAndroidHarmonyOS

The HarmonyOS app has no HostTap account and no server of ours. It sends control input directly to the computer you paired with, collects no personal information, and includes no advertising, tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

01What this policy covers

This policy covers the HarmonyOS app published by GUIHUI HU through AppGallery, with bundle name org.kakahu.hosttap. It describes what the app does on your phone only.

The computer side is a separate program; its practices are described in the general HostTap privacy policy. This policy also does not cover future services, or the privacy practices of your keyboard, speech-recognition provider, or the applications you control.

02Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Name, phone number, email address, or any other identity information;
  • Location information of any kind;
  • Contacts, messages, call records, photos, files, or clipboard content;
  • Device identifiers such as IMEI, OAID, serial number, or advertising ID;
  • App usage behaviour, crash logs, or diagnostic analytics.

There is no sign-in, no user profile, and no HostTap-operated server that receives your data.

03Permissions the app requests

The app declares two ordinary permissions. Both are used only to reach the computer you paired with.

PermissionWhy it is neededWhat leaves your phone
ohos.permission.INTERNET Opens the encrypted connection to your computer and carries mouse, keyboard, and text input Only the control input you produce, sent directly to that computer. No relay or publisher-operated server is involved.
ohos.permission.GET_NETWORK_INFO Tells whether a usable network is present, so the app can reconnect to your computer Nothing. The app does not read the network name, the Wi-Fi password, or your traffic.

The app requests no permission for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone state, storage, or background running.

04Pairing with the scanning service

Pairing starts when you scan the QR code shown on your computer. The app calls the system scanning service to do this, so it does not request camera permission: the system handles the camera preview and hands the app only the decoded text.

The app does not receive, store, or upload any image or video. You can leave the system scanning screen at any time to stop scanning, and the app scans only when you open the scanner yourself.

05Information stored on your phone

To spare you from pairing again every time, the app keeps the following on your phone:

ContentWhere it is keptNotes
Saved computers: name, address, port, and TLS certificate fingerprint The app’s own preferences Up to 20 records; visible and removable in the app
Access credential for each paired computer The system Asset Store Encrypted and access-controlled by HarmonyOS; never written to an ordinary file
Your privacy-consent choice The app’s own preferences Records only that you accepted this policy and which version

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Removing a saved computer in the app deletes that record and its credential; uninstalling the app removes all of it. The app keeps no copy elsewhere, so there is no server-side data for you to request or delete.

06What is sent to your computer

The connection to your computer is protected as follows:

  • Input travels over TLS, and the app verifies the certificate fingerprint recorded at pairing; if it does not match, the connection is aborted;
  • The access credential is sent only after that verification succeeds;
  • The payload is mouse movement, clicks, key presses, and the text you deliberately send — including the text you submit in the text-send field and the edits you make in live editing.

That content goes only to the computer you paired with. It is not sent to us, and we keep no copy. Devices usually connect on the same local network; the actual route depends on your network setup.

In live editing the app receives the current text and selection of the supported input field on your computer and keeps that draft on the phone only. If the phone and the computer disagree, live editing stops instead of overwriting your text.

07AppGallery, this website, and support

AppGallery. Huawei distributes and updates this version of the app and may process distribution, installation, and diagnostic information under the Huawei Privacy Policy.

This website. Cloudflare Pages hosts this privacy page. Cloudflare processes network and request information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We add no advertising, tracking scripts, analytics, forms, or cookies of our own.

Support. If you email kakahu@kakahu.org, GUIHUI HU receives your address and whatever you choose to write, and uses them only to reply. Please do not send passwords, pairing links, QR codes, or private text. We keep support mail only as long as needed to handle your request, and you can email us to have it deleted.

08Sharing, and your choices

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because the app does not collect any. We do not use your input to train AI. Information you voluntarily email to us may be disclosed only if the law requires it.

Within the app you can remove saved computers, disconnect at any time, and leave the scanner to stop scanning. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. To withdraw consent, simply stop using the app and uninstall it.

09Children

The app is a utility tool and is not directed at children. It collects no age-related information and contains no advertising or content aimed at minors.

10Changes and contact

If a future version of the app handles information differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be announced inside the app before they take effect.

For privacy questions or requests, contact GUIHUI HU at kakahu@kakahu.org. We reply within 15 business days.
