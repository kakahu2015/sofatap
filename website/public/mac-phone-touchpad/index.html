Mac setup

Use your phone as a touchpad and keyboard for Mac

HostTap connects a phone to the receiver on your Mac so you can move the pointer, click, scroll and enter text while watching the Mac display.

What you need

  • The HostTap Mac receiver package that matches your processor: Apple silicon or Intel.
  • The HostTap Android app or KeyTrans for iPhone, which includes HostTap controls.
  • A network route that lets the phone and Mac reach each other. HostTap does not use a cloud relay.

Pair a phone with your Mac

  1. Download the Apple silicon or Intel ZIP from the HostTap downloads page, then extract it.
  2. Open the HostTap receiver on your Mac and follow any macOS permission prompt needed for pointer and keyboard control.
  3. Open HostTap on Android or KeyTrans on iPhone and choose the QR scanning option.
  4. Scan the pairing code shown by the Mac receiver and confirm the Mac in the phone app.
  5. Use the touch surface to move and click the pointer. Open the phone keyboard when you want to type.

If the phone cannot connect

Check that both devices can reach each other over the network. Guest Wi-Fi, device isolation, or a restrictive firewall can block direct connections. HostTap does not provide a cloud relay.

If macOS asks for permission to control the pointer or keyboard, approve it in System Settings and return to the receiver. Pairing and input control are separate steps.

Get HostTap

Choose the Mac package for your processor, then install the companion app on your phone.
